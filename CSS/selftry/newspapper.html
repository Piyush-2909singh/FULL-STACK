Vol. CXXIV No. 42 THE DAILY CHRONICLE Friday, August 20, 2026

Europe's Summer Reading List

BERLIN — European researchers successfully tested a low-cost, grid-scale iron-air battery on Friday that can store power for up to 100 hours at a fraction of the cost of lithium-ion systems. The breakthrough, detailed in the journal Nature Energy, addresses a major bottleneck for renewable energy by providing cheap, long-duration backup power when wind and solar output drops.
