POKEMON GENERATION

Kanto Starters

The first generation introduces the original 151 Pokémon and the iconic Kanto region. A classic start to every trainer's journey.

Johto Additions

Generation 2 expands the world with new Pokémon, evolutions, and the Johto region.

Hoenn Adventures

Hoenn brings diverse environments and new mechanics like abilities and natures.

Sinnoh Era

Sinnoh expands the story and introduces many fan-favorite Pokémon and evolutions.

Unova Innovations

Unova introduces a larger set of new Pokémon and refined visuals for the series.
