⚡ POKEDEX ENTRIES ⚡

The morning fog clung to the edges of the quiet town, softening the outlines of buildings and trees alike. A lone cyclist pedaled through the mist, the rhythmic sound of wheels on wet pavement echoing faintly in the still air. Birds chirped intermittently, their songs muffled by the haze, while the scent of damp earth and blooming flowers mingled in the breeze. Somewhere in the distance, a bell tolled, marking the start of a new day, and the town slowly began to stir, each movement carrying the promise of unexpected encounters and small adventures.
