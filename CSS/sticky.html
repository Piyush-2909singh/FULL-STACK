POKEMON GENERATIONS

Kanto Starters

The first generation introduces the original 151 Pokémon and the iconic Kanto region. Featuring legendary starters like Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle, Generation 1 created the foundation of the Pokémon world and began countless trainers' journeys.

Johto Additions

Generation 2 expands the Pokémon universe with the beautiful Johto region and introduces new Pokémon, evolutions, abilities, and the day and night system. Pokémon like Chikorita, Cyndaquil, and Totodile became unforgettable additions to the series.

Hoenn Adventures

Hoenn brings a completely different environment filled with oceans, mountains, forests, and volcanic landscapes. Generation 3 introduced important mechanics such as abilities, natures, and double battles, making Pokémon battles more strategic than ever.

Sinnoh Era

Generation 4 takes trainers to the Sinnoh region and introduces some of the most popular Pokémon in the series. With powerful legendary Pokémon, new evolutions, and an expanded storyline, Sinnoh became one of the most memorable generations for fans.

Unova Innovations

Generation 5 introduces the Unova region and a completely new collection of Pokémon. The games focused strongly on story and identity while bringing improved visuals, animated battles, and a fresh experience for trainers.
